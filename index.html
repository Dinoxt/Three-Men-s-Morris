HOW TO PLAY

  • 1. Placement Phase: Players take turns placing their 3 pieces on any empty dot on the board.
  • 2. Movement Phase: Once all 3 pieces are placed, take turns sliding one of your pieces along a connected line to an adjacent empty dot.
  • 3. Winning: Be the first to align all 3 of your pieces in a straight row (horizontally, vertically, or diagonally)!
